Coverage and source checks

The dashboard links resources across California. Project inventories vary by city. No citywide inventory is currently certified complete. A list of annual housing reports, environmental filings or selected major projects is partial coverage, even when every row of that source has been imported.

Historical applications and alternative proposals can share an address. Record counts are not counts of distinct developments or homes. A source-check date records a review of the linked material, not a guarantee that all permits or construction activity are current.
